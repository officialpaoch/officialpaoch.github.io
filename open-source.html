Open product data

Open Beauty Facts

When you scan a barcode, PAOCH may query the community-powered Open Beauty Facts database for product details. The service is powered by the open-source Product Opener project.

Its database is available under the Open Database License, individual database contents under the Database Contents License, and product images under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike. Learn more in the official reuse and licensing guide.
