Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Paoch ("the App") is a local-first cosmetic tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what information stays on your device, what limited information is processed by third-party services used by the App, and the choices available to you.

Information You Provide

You may enter product names, brands, categories, dates, notes, costs, ratings, routines, reminder settings, and optional personalization preferences. You may also capture or select product photos. This user-created content is stored locally in the App's sandbox on your device. Paoch does not operate an account system or cloud-sync service and does not receive this content.

If you export a backup or share a pouch image, the App gives the file to your device's share interface. You choose the destination and are responsible for the service or person with whom you share it.

Information Processed Automatically

Paoch does not operate its own analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting service. The App does, however, include the following third-party SDKs:

  • Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (AdMob/UMP): The App initializes Google's advertising and consent SDKs on iOS and may show banner ads to free users. Paoch requests non-personalized ads and does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Google may still process an IP address (which can indicate general location), device or app identifiers, advertising data, interactions with the App and ads, diagnostic information, and performance information for contextual advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. UMP presents privacy choices where required, Paoch requests ads only when the SDK reports that ads may be requested, and an in-App privacy-options entry point appears when UMP requires one.
  • RevenueCat: The App uses RevenueCat to offer and manage Paoch Pro purchases. RevenueCat processes an SDK-generated anonymous App User ID, purchase history, Apple receipt and transaction information, product and entitlement status, and technical information such as device type, operating system, locale, currency, and IP address. Paoch does not send RevenueCat your name or email address because the App has no account system. Apple processes your payment method; Paoch and RevenueCat do not receive your full payment card details.

Other Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store: Apple processes purchases, refunds, subscription management, and payment information under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Open Beauty Facts: When you scan or enter a barcode, the App sends that barcode and the technical information inherent in an internet request, such as your IP address, to the Open Beauty Facts API to retrieve product information. Paoch does not include your profile, photos, or product notes in this request.
  • UPCitemdb: If Open Beauty Facts cannot return a usable product, the App sends the barcode directly to UPCitemdb's API to retrieve product information. This request exposes your IP address and standard request metadata to UPCitemdb. It does not include your profile, photos, product notes, or RevenueCat App User ID.
  • Amazon links: When you choose to open an Amazon product-search link, the App passes the product name and brand as search terms to your external browser. Amazon and your browser process that request under their own policies. The link may contain an affiliate tag, and Paoch may earn a commission from a resulting purchase.

The relevant third-party policies are available from Google, RevenueCat, Apple, Open Beauty Facts, UPCitemdb, and Amazon.

International Processing

The third-party providers listed above may process information outside the country where you use the App:

  • Google and RevenueCat process information in the United States and may use infrastructure or subprocessors in other countries.
  • RevenueCat uses United States-based cloud infrastructure to maintain purchase and entitlement records.
  • Open Beauty Facts is operated from France.
  • UPCitemdb receives barcode lookup requests directly from your device. Its privacy contact is in the United States. For information about its processing locations and international transfers, contact privacy@upcitemdb.com.
  • Apple and Amazon process information in the countries described in their respective policies.

For South Korean users, these overseas providers receive only the information described in this Policy for advertising and consent management, purchase and entitlement management, barcode lookup, or a link you chose to open. By using a feature that requires one of these providers, you request the associated processing. Where applicable law requires a separate choice, the App or the provider will present one before the processing occurs.

Device Permissions

Paoch may request:

  • Camera: To scan barcodes and take product photos.
  • Photo Library: To select existing product images.
  • Motion: To animate the pouch while you use the App. Motion samples are not stored.
  • Notifications: To create local product-expiration reminders. Paoch does not use a remote push-notification service.

You can grant or deny these permissions and change them later in iOS Settings.

Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Products, routines, preferences, and images are stored locally until you delete them, clear App data, or uninstall the App. Backup files remain wherever you choose to save or share them.

Google, RevenueCat, Apple, Open Beauty Facts, UPCitemdb, Amazon, and any destination you choose through the share interface retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. Purchase records may need to be retained to validate entitlements, process refunds, prevent fraud, and meet financial or legal requirements.

UPCitemdb's privacy policy describes storage of IP addresses and technical request logs, but does not specify a fixed retention period for anonymous API lookup logs. Clearing data in Paoch or uninstalling the App does not delete records retained by third-party providers. For requests about UPCitemdb's records, contact privacy@upcitemdb.com.

To request help with information processed on Paoch's behalf by a service provider, contact us at the address below. We may need an anonymous purchase identifier or other details to locate the relevant record.

Your Choices and Rights

You can review or change advertising privacy choices from the App's Data & Privacy settings whenever UMP requires that option. You can delete individual items or use Clear All Data for locally stored App content, and you can export a backup before deletion. You may also disable permissions in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent. Contact us to exercise a right relating to information processed on Paoch's behalf. We will respond as required by applicable law and may direct you to Apple or another provider when that provider controls the relevant record.

Children's Privacy

Paoch is not directed to children under 14. We do not knowingly use the App to collect personal information from children under 14 or knowingly enable behavior-based advertising to them. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party service used by the App, contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact officialpaoch@gmail.com.
